# Nigerian Upstream Oil & Gas Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end analysis of Nigerian upstream oil & gas operations — cleaned in Excel with Power Query, validated with pivot tables, and visualized in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Business Question

> How can Nigerian upstream oil & gas operators improve production efficiency and revenue while minimizing operational downtime, production losses, operating costs, and gas flaring?

**Supporting questions:**
- Which operators and oil fields contribute the most to total crude oil production?
- What operational issues are responsible for the highest downtime?
- Which operators generate the highest estimated revenue?
- Which operators produce the most gas?

## Tools

- **Microsoft Excel** — Power Query for data cleaning, PivotTables for KPI validation
- **Power BI** — interactive dashboard and visualization

## Process

1. **Raw Data** — messy operator-level dataset, inconsistent naming and unstructured fields
2. **Power Query Cleaning** — standardized fields and numeric types across operators and oil fields
3. **Pivot Table Validation** — cross-checked KPI totals before visualizing
4. **Power BI Dashboard** — built KPI cards and charts for production, operations, financial, and environmental performance

#
**Key metrics:** Total Oil Production (99.68M bbl) · Total Revenue (₦7.93bn) · Total Gas Production (175.63M Mscf) · Gas Flare Rate (7.62%)

## Key Insights

- **Eni** leads oil production at 11.01M bbl, narrowly ahead of **NNPC** at 10.91M bbl
- **Ogoniland** is the top-producing field (10.45M bbl); **Escravos** is lowest (6.70M bbl)
- **Pipeline outages** account for 37% of all downtime — the single largest operational issue
- **NNPC** generates the highest revenue (₦852.8M) despite Eni producing more oil, pointing to a stronger realized revenue mix
- The **gas flare rate stands at 7.62%** — 13.39M of 175.63M Mscf produced is flared rather than captured

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize pipeline integrity — targeted monitoring and preventive maintenance for the largest efficiency gain
2. Study and replicate NNPC and Eni's operating practices across lower-performing operators
3. Invest in gas capture infrastructure to reduce the 7.62% flare rate, starting with the highest-volume producers
4. Reassess underperforming fields such as Escravos and Bonga for cost-effective recovery or reallocated investment
5. Track revenue-per-barrel by operator, not just volume, to close monetization gaps
